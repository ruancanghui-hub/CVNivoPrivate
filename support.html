常见问题

  • 如何删除数据？打开应用 → 我的 → 本机空间 →「清除数据 / 删除账户」，按提示确认。
  • AI 会不会乱写履历？AI 只重组你已填写的事实；写回前必须你确认。
  • 为什么有广告？部分能力通过激励广告解锁；也可能出现开屏广告。无内购。

FAQ

  • How do I delete data? Me → On-device space → Clear data / Delete account, then confirm.
  • Will AI invent my history? No. AI only reorganizes facts you entered; write-back requires your confirmation.
  • Why ads? Some features unlock via rewarded ads; an app-open ad may appear. No IAPs.
